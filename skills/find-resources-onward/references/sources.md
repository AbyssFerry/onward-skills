# Source Map

Use these discovery entry points according to their role. A site may contain several kinds of resources. For a specific field, creators, professional organizations, and communities often provide more relevant leads than general directories.

## Choosing research areas

These areas describe the kinds of help the source groups can provide. Use them as starting points, and extend or reshape them to fit the task.

| Research area | What it can contribute |
|---|---|
| Skills, plugins, and MCP | Capabilities an agent can add or connect. |
| Software, code, models, and data | Existing tools, reusable implementations, models, and datasets. |
| Official docs, standards, and learning materials | Authoritative rules, supported behavior, and structured explanations. |
| Original articles, blogs, and methods | Methods, tutorials, workflows, and first-hand lessons. |
| Communities and emerging leads | Experiences from users, practical problems, recommendations, and emerging resources. |
| Cases, templates, assets, and inspiration | Comparable work, reusable starting points, and visual or workflow ideas. |
| Academic and professional research | Studies, evidence syntheses, and specialist literature. |

## Skills, plugins, and MCP

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [skills.sh](https://skills.sh/) | Search skills by task, capability, or author, then follow the result to its upstream repository. |
| [SkillsMP](https://skillsmp.com/) | Broaden skill discovery with another index and set of categories; vary keywords, authors, and categories. |
| [Tencent SkillHub / 腾讯 SkillHub](https://skillhub.cn/) | Find skills for Chinese-language and domestic use cases; its [source repository and docs](https://github.com/Tencent/skillhub) offer another entry point. |
| Plugin Management in the current environment | Search available host plugins by product or capability. The catalog may vary by host and account. |
| [GitHub Agent Skills topic](https://github.com/topics/agent-skills) | Find independent skills and author collections beyond aggregators; GitHub search can also include task terms and `SKILL.md`. |
| [OpenAI Skills](https://github.com/openai/skills) and [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) | Explore author collections and follow related authors, references, and projects. |
| [MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/) | Look up server registrations, versions, and publishers. Its [API docs](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/registry/blob/main/docs/reference/api/official-registry-api.md) help when programmatic lookup is useful. |
| [Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers) and [PulseMCP](https://www.pulsemcp.com/servers) | Find MCP servers by capability, category, or tool description, then follow links to the original publisher. |

## Software, code, models, and data

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [GitHub](https://github.com/search) and [GitLab](https://gitlab.com/explore/projects) | Repositories, CLIs, MCP servers, templates, and comparable projects. Topics, READMEs, examples, issues, and releases offer different clues. |
| [Gitee / OSS Compass](https://compass.gitee.com/explore) | Chinese open-source projects, categories, and alternatives; follow listings to upstream repositories. |
| [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/) and [PyPI](https://pypi.org/) | Packages and CLIs. Search by task, command, or language, then check the author's docs and supported environment. |
| [WinGet](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/package-manager/winget/search) | Windows apps and CLIs. Where WinGet is available, search by name, ID, or command; other operating systems have their own package indexes. |
| [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) | Models, datasets, and Spaces. Task tags, authors, model/dataset cards, and demos help establish intended use. |
| [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) | New software, services, and product directions; browse by category or problem, then check the product site. |

## Official docs, standards, and learning materials

Start with the product, platform, standards body, professional organization, or university relevant to the task. Search its terminology, capability names, and versions. Technical docs, formal standards, and learning explanations serve different purposes.

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) | Skill format and compatibility requirements. |
| [OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/) and [Codex Skills docs](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills) | OpenAI products and APIs, Codex capabilities, integrations, and skill behavior. |
| [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web) and [web.dev](https://web.dev/learn/) | Web technology explanations, compatibility information, learning materials, and practical guides. |
| [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/) | Microsoft products, platforms, services, and learning paths; search by product and capability. |
| [W3C](https://www.w3.org/TR/) | Formal Web standards; explanatory articles can lead to the corresponding standard. |

This list does not cover every field. For other topics, look for the relevant authorities, professional associations, research institutions, publishers, specialist databases, and the original sources they cite.

## Original articles, blogs, and methods

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/) | UX research and methods; search by topic, method, or author. |
| [Smashing Magazine](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/) | Design and front-end practice; browse topics or search for a specific problem and its original author. |
| [Stripe Blog](https://stripe.com/blog) and [Shopify Engineering](https://shopify.engineering/latest) | First-hand engineering practice, decision context, and implementation stories. |
| [掘金 Juejin](https://juejin.cn/) and [知乎 Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/) | Chinese tutorials, experience, and comparisons. Search specific questions and technical terms; check the author, date, and citations. |

These are examples rather than a complete blog list. Community discussions, project READMEs, release notes, and bibliographies often lead to more relevant writers and specialist publications. Try combinations such as `topic + workflow / guide / case study / lessons learned`, along with Chinese-language terms.

## Communities and emerging leads

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [X / Twitter](https://x.com/search) | Author announcements, new tools, and workflows. Search by capability, author, or project and follow posts to original sources. |
| [Linux.do](https://linux.do/) | Chinese-language experience with skills, MCP, CLIs, and tools; search topics and follow links in discussions. |
| [V2EX](https://www.v2ex.com/) | Tool recommendations, product feedback, and Chinese-language project discussions; browse nodes or use `site:v2ex.com topic`. |
| [Hacker News Search](https://hn.algolia.com/) | Show HN, Ask HN, author write-ups, and alternatives; search both project names and task terms. |
| [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/search/) | Field communities, user experience, and resource lists; identify relevant subreddits and explore them. |

Community posts suggest candidates and questions; original publisher materials help establish what a resource does. See [Access Methods](access-methods.md) when a site is hard to reach.

## Cases, templates, assets, and inspiration

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [Figma Community](https://www.figma.com/community) | Templates, design assets, and plugins; search by resource type, task, or creator. |
| [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) and [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/) | Product screens and flows; search by product, platform, or task. |
| [Behance](https://www.behance.net/) and [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots) | Work, design stories, and visual directions; identify the parts relevant to the task. |
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/) and [SiteInspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/) | Website visuals and interaction ideas; browse by style, industry, or technology and inspect the original site. |
| [Microsoft templates](https://word.cloud.microsoft/create/en/templates/) | Document structures and layouts; search by document purpose. |

Code examples can also be found through the repository sources above. Finished work, process case studies, and reusable implementations serve different needs; choose according to what the user wants to learn or adapt.

## Academic and professional research

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| [Consensus](https://consensus.app/) | Discover scientific papers with natural-language queries and find cited evidence syntheses. When the Consensus plugin is available, its search can find papers and its research agent can synthesize multi-part questions. |
| [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/) | Preprints and research methods; search by title, author, field, and version. |
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Biomedical literature; filter with subject terms and publication types. |
| [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/) | DOI and publication metadata; follow citations to original papers, reports, or books. |

Search, login, subscription, and API terms can change. This map provides entry points and directions; report what was actually searched for each task.
