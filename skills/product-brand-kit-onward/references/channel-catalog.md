# Campaign Channel Catalog

Use the approved visual direction and the same set of source assets across applicable channels. Start with the first placement. If final dimensions are missing, use a representative ratio and label it as a preview in the index.

V01–V06 identify the master assets. A01–A07 identify independently usable campaign pieces. V06 preserves the reusable subject, text area, and composition. A01 turns it into a poster for an actual readership, setting, and size.

## Check paper and packaging together

For a fictional tea gift, show the brand and tea descriptor on the lid, arrange the gift line as the first thing visible on opening, and test a side panel and one pouch or card. Show the card's actual location in the A07 preview while delivering its editable A05 artwork separately with the writing area marked. Identify approved identity elements and proposed placement choices.

Group product hero images, variants, and detail graphics under A03. Group display cards, vouchers, and gift notes under A05. Use A06 for unboxing frames and captions, A01 for banners or seasonal signs, and A02 for social covers. Give multiple pieces under an ID readable suffixes, such as `A07-box-front` and `A07-pouch`. Show common rules and a representative set first. Produce and index each part the user expressly requested.

## Deliver a full set or an individual asset

Batch the applicable IDs once a direction is established. Give each asset a specific placement note. When the audience, message, and visual direction are settled, handle routine channel dimensions and formats as design work. Where an ID is inapplicable, a brief note in the index is sufficient. Use readable variants such as `A02-cover-vertical`. Supply easily found finished files and editable sources, with PDF thumbnails where they help selection.

- **“Make only a mail-order poster for Still Hour.”** Use the approved logo, descriptor, and contextual line. Keep the brand name and text legible at the agreed size. Deliver only A01 and the supporting source it needs.
- **“Make the event landing page. I only have an idea and a name.”** Ask for facts that affect the first screen. Create an independently viewable A03 centered on the idea and audience. Preserve room for the date and venue once they are confirmed.
- **“Update this tagline only on social.”** Change the affected A02 graphics, copy, and index entry. Flag other uses of the previous V02 line in the index so they are easy to find.

## Check the actual placements

Check posters at their viewing distance and mobile covers and avatars at their intended size. For different ratios, review safe text areas, subject crops, and logo spacing separately. Recompose the hierarchy before resizing type. Check that storyboards read without sound, paper works at actual size, pages read on a phone, and distributed files carry the intended name and attribution. Open local resource links before handoff.
