# Ecommerce Content and Listings

Use this reference for separately requested listings and sales materials. Use the [channel catalog](channel-catalog.md) for the campaign's A03 brand page and ecommerce visuals. The C identifiers below are optional commerce deliverables. Choose what the assignment actually requires.

## Establish what a customer can buy or do

| Offer | Facts that affect the page | Appropriate next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stocked, made-to-order, or presold physical product | Variant, included items, price and currency, availability, destination, shipping cost, timing, and returns | Select a variant and buy when the transaction is ready. State applicable preorder terms. |
| Digital product or subscription | Supported device, activation, feature scope, billing period, trial, renewal, and cancellation | Download, trial, select a plan, or subscribe through an available route. |
| In-person service or consultation | Eligible customers, area, included work, rates or quote process, booking, seller, and cancellation | Check availability, book, or contact sales. |
| Concept or expression of interest | What can be demonstrated now, what is being tested, whether registration is open, and what happens next | View a demo, express interest, or book an interview, based on actual readiness. |

If the brief includes commercial feasibility, source revenue, variable fulfillment and payment costs, platform fees, refunds, and acquisition costs. Mark untested figures as hypotheses. Use actual order or repeat purchase records when they are available.

Maintain one product record for brand, category, name, line, use, supportable features, variants, and asset sources. Maintain prices, stock, delivery, promotions, and after-sales terms by channel, market, variant, and effective date. Record each field's source or owner, date, and whether it is public. Keep market-specific changes out of evergreen identity materials.

An internal SKU, product barcode, and deliverable ID serve different purposes. Map each variant to its own images, price, stock, and purchase option. Put unassigned identifiers, links, certifications, ratings, or counts in the internal gap list. Complete usable discovery materials and record missing transaction facts in affected files. [Shopify product details](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details)

## Deliver the applicable pieces independently

Use openable pages, editable visual sources, copy-ready text, field tables, or shot lists as appropriate. Record platform fields that still need verification.

| ID | Deliverable | What to walk through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 Home page | Main promise, product or range entries, trusted reasons to explore | A first visitor knows what is offered and can find the right product. |
| C02 Brand or product introduction | Why it exists, how it works, who uses it, related brands or products, and next step | The page makes sense by itself, and the supporting facts are traceable. |
| C03 Product detail page | Title, leading images, evidenced benefits, variants, inclusions, price, fulfillment, support, and a working next step | On mobile, the customer can choose, know what arrives and when, and find help. Changing variants updates the details. |
| C04 Listing and search results card | Category, title, attributes, benefits, images and purpose, variants, short description | The card identifies the brand, category, and variant, and opens the same product. Field values follow current official rules. |
| C05 Range comparison | Compare actual products on common criteria and link to the right offer for each situation | Differences are supportable and shoppers can tell when to pick the featured product. |
| C06 Social post | Ready-to-use headline, text, image sequence, captions, and destination | Each image or panel has a purpose. The post answers the question or scene it opens with. |
| C07 Short video and C08 Live session | Separate scripts. Include timing, shots, action, narration, screen text, or a live demonstration with variant and question handling | Read the copy aloud, identify real or pending assets, and check every price or campaign claim. |
| C09 Advertisement | Layout-ready title, copy, image, call to action, landing page, and one proposition to test | The advertised product or condition is immediately visible on the linked page. |
| C10 Community or direct message and C11 Email or notification | Separate scripts by recipient and moment. For email include subject, preview, body, and link. Base transaction notices on actual events | Confirm template variables, audience, links, and who is meant to receive it. |
| C12 FAQ and customer service | Customer-worded questions, short answers, terms, and escalation route | Support can answer fit, cost, and returns consistently with the detail page. |
| C16 First use | Actual unboxing, setup, activation, or booking steps with exception handling and help | A new customer can complete the first task. Substantiate instructions about safety or storage. |
| C18 Product feed | Platform-mapped data for each eligible variant, with missing fields tracked internally | IDs are unique. Images, price, currency, availability, landing page, and checkout match. |

Batch relevant pieces using the same evidence. When a sales condition changes, trace affected pages, ads, support copy, and feeds by product, market, variant, and deliverable ID.

## Adapt to the channel

- For a retail marketplace, make the title identify the item and give the most useful details in images and scannable copy. Check the site's current category and listing rules. [Amazon product detail guidance](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/improve-your-products-for-advertising)
- Choose an actual buying question for each image. A photo in hand can show scale. A screen sequence can demonstrate a real task. Put verified shipping costs or calculation conditions near the decision. [Baymard product-page research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux)
- For feeds, verify identifiers, variants, price, stock, images, and destination against the current rules for the merchant and market. For Google, check the structured fields for AI-generated titles and descriptions and the applicable image provenance metadata. [Google Merchant Center product data](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) · [Google landing page requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/4752265?hl=en)
- On Douyin Commerce, check the real seller, product category, and region. Record the rule title, effective date, and official link in the listing sheet. Use the merchant's export or screenshot for requirements available only from the account. [Douyin Commerce rules](https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/rules/nHVrw6XLZLB5)
- Give a video one opening action, a demonstration, and a next step. Recheck aspect ratios and subtitle safety after adaptation. For email, let the subject identify the message and the preview text explain why to open it. [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Creative_Codes_ENG.pdf) · [Mailchimp preview text](https://mailchimp.com/help/about-preview-text/)

For fictional A5 and A6 notebooks, the home page helps visitors choose by setting, the comparison shows devices or hands for scale, and the detail page provides exact dimensions. A video can demonstrate a tear-off page. If shipping is undecided, record it as a gap in the detail page, platform listing, and feed while the other content proceeds to review.

Once the offer is live, identify which step is losing people within the same channel, region, variant, and period. Verify collected events and whether the denominator is sessions or users before interpreting visits, carts, checkout, purchases, or bookings. Check specifications, price, trust, and calls to action when visits do not turn into carts. Check payment and shipping when people start checking out but stop. Record an observation and the next change in the index. [Google Analytics ecommerce purchase report](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12924131?hl=en)
