# Mainland China Trademark and Asset Permissions

Consult this guide for naming, carrying over a mark, co-branding, opening a venue, or adding categories in mainland China. Confirm who trades, who may use each asset, and which goods or services and territory are in scope. Use the applicable authority for other markets.

Check the law, implementing regulations, and transition rules in force at the time of the work. The 2019 amended Trademark Law is the current text as of 21 November 2026. Article 87 of the 2026 revision sets its effective date to 1 January 2027. [2019 amended Trademark Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html) · [2026 revised Trademark Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2026/6/26/art_95_206942.html)

## Record who owns, uses, and supplies what

| Ask the owner | Record and apply |
| --- | --- |
| What will people see, and who actually sells or supplies it? | Record the full legal name, public brand, series or product, and store name. Verify seller, contracting entity, and support where they are displayed. |
| Who applied for or owns each name and sign? | Record applicant or registrant, application or registration number, status, approved items, market, and verification date per mark. Treat domain, company, and social account names as separate identifiers. |
| Who may use each other party's name or logo? | Record licensee, territory, term, covered goods or services, channels, and permissions to alter or sublicense. Verify each public relationship claim and check platform qualifications for labels such as flagship store. |
| Who created each asset? | Record creator, source, contract or license, approved use, and permission to distribute editable files for logos, illustrations, type, photos, and music. Verify asset rights separately from possession of a source file. |

Record the applicant's identity as shown on supporting documents and distinguish it from the operating licensee. For a trademark license or assignment, verify which contract, application, quality control, disclosure, filing, and announcement provisions apply. For commissioned creative work, check the copyright contract and the statutory ownership rule when the contract is silent. Review rights by medium, term, territory, and right to supply editable sources. Recheck when extending website imagery to ads, packaging, or a retailer asset pack. [Trademark Law](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/30/art_95_28179.html) · [Implementing Regulations](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zfjcj/tzgg/art/2023/art_ded7a0639f184377ba7b515cb0358db2.html) · [Copyright Law, Articles 19 and 26–29](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/2871/2872/69475.html)

## Map actual activities to the goods and services

1. Describe each activity in terms of who does what for whom. Separate current activity from planned activity. A fictional tea maker can sell packaged tea now and plan to serve customers at a tasting venue later.
2. Check the version of the classification in use for the application. Identify the specific goods or services, class, relevant similarity group, and notes. Record the scope of each search. A class number is an index. Assessment also depends on the signs, specific goods or services, and earlier rights. [WIPO Nice Classification](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/classification-nice)
3. Distinguish a business selling its own products from one providing promotion or an online marketplace for others. Start with the actual goods or services and check the relevant wording and platform requirements separately. [CNIPA Class 35 guidance](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2022/12/7/art_66_180686.html)

For example, a check of text names for the packaged product leaves the logo and the future in-person service unassessed. Say which date, visuals, goods, and search pages the file covers.

## Move from names to channel artwork

| Stage | Action and record |
| --- | --- |
| Name and sketch | Record intended text, Romanization or translations where relevant, signs, and combinations. Check for descriptiveness, prohibited elements, misleading origin or quality, and relevant earlier rights. Keep viable alternatives. |
| Preliminary search | Record the date, market, official search source, terms, visual elements, relevant goods and services, hits, and gaps. Use dated wording such as “no result under these terms on this date” when appropriate. A text-only query leaves the symbol unchecked. |
| Confirm status | Track a working name, limited search, application or acceptance, preliminary publication, and registration as distinct evidence-backed states. Verify the registered form, scope, and permission before public use. Refer ambiguous scope, conflicts, clearance decisions, or filing to the rights holder or qualified adviser with the evidence. Continue concept work with a clearly marked working identity when appropriate. |
| Export | Use a plain mark for design drafts. Before displaying ®, verify active registration, approved artwork, goods or services, and authorization. For marks on goods or associated materials, consult the applicable placement rule. Recheck when the artwork, combination, category, or license term changes. |

The implementing regulations identify the upper-right or lower-right placement of a registration symbol under Article 63. Verify which rules apply for the use date. Public claims and formal judgments require evidence of the current status. [Implementing Regulations, Article 63](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zfjcj/tzgg/art/2023/art_ded7a0639f184377ba7b515cb0358db2.html)

## Preserve evidence and propagate changes

In the internal material, record asset or name, owner and user, territory and covered goods or services, rights and term, dated evidence, linked assets, and outstanding owner actions. Keep private agreements in the appropriate access-controlled location. Use only verified credits and permitted materials in public exports. Use the linked IDs to update images, pages, and PDFs when names or rights change. After publication, keep dated records of actual use that connect the entity, mark, and goods or services, such as pages, transactions, or product photos. Keep concept renderings clearly identified.
