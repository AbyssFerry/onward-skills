# Brand Identity and Campaign Kit

Turn an idea into words people can repeat, a mark they can recognize, and visuals ready for the intended channels. A first-time viewer can tell who is speaking and what they are saying. The person taking over can find the exact asset or copy they need. When the user requests a method, concept review, or individual asset, deliver at that level.

## Start with the idea

Review existing names, wording, sketches, photos, and approved work. Ask only about gaps that would materially change the language or visuals.

- **Who should remember what, and when?** Describe one person and one moment. For a quality like warmth, find an action that would make someone feel it.
- **Who is speaking?** Decide which brand, event, organizer, or venue should be recognized first. Record exact spellings, marks, people, and artwork that must carry over. Confirm relationships that affect attribution.
- **What should it feel and sound like?** Identify what works in the references, such as a letterform, color, space, voice, or composition. Identify which decisions the user wants to make and which they delegate.
- **Where will it appear first?** Pick a representative placement such as a storefront poster, phone cover, or gift insert. Suggest a plausible setting when the starting point is just an idea.

Condense the answers into a creative brief. Distinguish approved language, facts suitable for publication, design decisions, and open questions. Proceed when the brief already answers the relevant questions. Use a vision or invitation for a concept, and support statements about actual events, relationships, or results with evidence.

## Set the direction with words and visuals together

Use [the brand workshop](references/brand-workshop.md). Make a visible direction board with the name or working name, a lead line, a story opening, a recognizable logo sketch, and a trial layout for a real placement. If routes would lead to materially different outcomes, show them in the same placement and explain what each choice changes. Develop an established direction or make the choice when the user delegates it.

For a full kit, use these identifiers. Reuse existing names and marks where appropriate and show how they will appear in this campaign.

| ID | Deliverable | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V01 Name and naming | Exact name, spelling, descriptor at first mention, and any secondary attribution | A new reader can identify the name and what it refers to. |
| V02 Tagline and contextual copy | A durable brand line and copy for actual contexts such as an invitation or a gift note | Each line has a placement and a speaker. The poster, card, and page use a coherent voice. |
| V03 Brand story and introduction | A standalone story and a short description ready to use | A concrete action, scene, or belief anchors the story. The short version works on its own. |
| V04 Logo | Existing mark and usage, or an original symbol or wordmark and its primary lockup, with editable master and transparent export | Spelling is accurate. The mark stays recognizable in the intended backgrounds, crops, and small sizes. |
| V05 Visual foundations | Color swatches and values, type and weight, hierarchy, imagery, and supporting graphics | A sample uses real copy and shows what each color, type style, and graphic does. |
| V06 Key visual | One independently usable composition with a focal point, lead line, mark, and clear hierarchy | The brand and message read without explanation. The composition retains recognizable elements across formats. |

## Extend the direction into campaign assets

Use [the channel catalog](references/channel-catalog.md) to produce the applicable assets in one batch once the direction is set. Complete every explicitly requested piece. For a single placement, deliver that asset and its necessary supporting files.

**A01 Main poster · A02 Social images and copy · A03 Brand page or ecommerce visuals · A04 Avatar and cover · A05 Print or gift collateral · A06 Video visuals or storyboard · A07 Packaging and unboxing.**

Adjust what the audience sees first, who is speaking, and how the image crops for each placement. V06 is the reusable composition. A01 is the finished poster for a specific audience, location, and size. Deliver an independently viewable page or the user's requested format for A03. Show actual keyframes for a storyboard. For a full kit with physical packaging or a gift box, include A07 by default. Show the front, key side or back panels, the first view on opening, and a representative accessory together. Supply separate flat artwork and editable sources. Apply the shared rules to one relevant accessory such as an insert, seal, pouch, label, or bag before adapting more pieces. Deliver actual artwork and finished copy with each applicable ID.

## Hand off the kit

Use [the delivery guide](references/delivery.md) for reusable copy, vector and transparent logo exports, the key visual and individual assets, editable sources, and the illustrated PDF for a full kit. Index each piece by placement, version, status, and relative path. Show the work before explaining its use. For a single item, hand over the finished asset, its editable source, and the usage notes needed to use it. Open the final files and check spelling, attribution, cropping, readability, and exports.
